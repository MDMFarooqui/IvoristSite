WHAT WE DO

Two sides of the same product;

A device is more than its circuit board. The firmware, software, and systems around it matter just as much.

Ivorist works across both hardware and software. That includes electronics and embedded systems, as well as the applications and services that connect everything together.

It means we can look at how the pieces will work together early on, rather than waiting until everything has been built to figure out how to connect them.
