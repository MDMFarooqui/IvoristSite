ABOUT IVORIST

Hardware and software belong together.

Ivorist is a small product development studio working across electronics, embedded systems, and software. We started it because we wanted to work on the whole product, not just one part of it.

WHY WE STARTED

The problem is often between the parts.

Hardware and software are often handled by separate teams. That isn't necessarily a problem, but it can become one when the two sides aren't involved in each other's decisions.

A board might be designed before the software requirements are fully understood. An application might be built without accounting for how the device actually behaves. These problems tend to appear later, when fixing them is more difficult.

Ivorist was started around a simpler approach: keep the hardware and software work connected from the beginning. The people working on the electronics should understand what the software needs, and the people writing the software should understand the device it depends on.

That's the kind of work we want to do: products where the different parts have been considered together, rather than stitched together at the end.

HOW WE APPROACH PROJECTS

A few things we keep in mind.

01

Understand the full product

We consider the device, its firmware, and the software around it before deciding how each part should be built.

02

Keep communication direct

The people doing the technical work should be part of the discussion. It makes it easier to spot problems and make decisions.

03

Test what we build

A design isn't finished just because it looks right on a screen. Prototypes and testing are part of the development process.
